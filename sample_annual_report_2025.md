# Example Corp A — Annual Report (Form 10-K)
**Fiscal Year Ended December 31, 2025**

---

## PART I

### Item 1. Business Overview
Example Corp A (the "Company") is a global enterprise software and cloud infrastructure provider founded in 2012. The Company specializes in secure cloud data platforms, AI-assisted business analytics, and scalable enterprise resource management solutions.

The Company operates through two primary reporting segments:
1. **Intelligent Cloud Platform:** Cloud compute infrastructure, managed database services, and enterprise security software.
2. **Business Productivity Solutions:** Subscription software for workflow automation, financial reporting, and collaboration.

During fiscal year 2025, the Company expanded operations into 12 new metropolitan regions and deepened strategic relationships with Fortune 500 enterprises.

---

### Item 1A. Risk Factors
Our business, financial condition, operating results, and cash flows are subject to various risks, including:

- **Intense Market Competition:** Rapid advancements in artificial intelligence and enterprise cloud technologies may shorten product adoption cycles. If we fail to innovate, competitors may capture market share.
- **Infrastructure and Capacity Investments:** Expanding data center capacity and localized compliance hubs requires significant capital commitments before associated revenues materialize.
- **Cybersecurity Disruption:** Security breaches or unauthorized data access could materially harm customer trust, disrupt cloud availability, and lead to regulatory fines.
- **Foreign Currency Volatility:** Because international revenue accounted for 35% of total sales in FY2025, fluctuations in the Euro and Yen relative to the U.S. Dollar create non-operational revenue variances.

## PART II

### Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations (MD&A)

#### Executive Overview
Fiscal year 2025 was marked by accelerated adoption of our Intelligent Cloud Platform. Total revenue grew from $100.0 million in fiscal year 2024 to $120.0 million in fiscal year 2025, representing a **20.0% year-over-year revenue increase**.

#### Operating Results & Expenses
Operating expenses increased proportionally from $60.0 million in FY2024 to $72.0 million in FY2025 (a **20.0% increase**). Management attributes this rise to three main drivers:
1. **Research & Development Headcount:** Aggressive engineering hiring for autonomous workflow engines and cloud data protection algorithms.
2. **Global Sales Channel Expansion:** Higher marketing outlays and direct field enablement in Europe and Asia-Pacific.
3. **Data Center Lease Commitments:** Increased facility fees associated with regional cloud cluster deployments.

#### Profitability and Margins Analysis
Net income rose from $15.0 million in FY2024 to $18.0 million in FY2025, achieving **20.0% growth**. 

However, despite substantial top-line and bottom-line expansion, our **Net Profit Margin remained constant at 15.0%** in both fiscal years (a change of **0.0 percentage points**). Because operating expenditures grew at the exact same 20.0% rate as revenues, operating leverage was neutral during the investment cycle. Management anticipates operational leverage gains will begin materializing in FY2026 as newly established regions achieve scale.
